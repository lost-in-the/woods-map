- The "Depends on" tile on a unit page no longer leaves dependencies off.
  - When a unit has more relationships than the tile's five rows, the last row collects the rest as "other".
    Before, relationships after the fifth were dropped.
    Which ones were dropped depended on the order the edges were published in.
  - Rows now order largest first.
    Relationships of equal size keep the tile's usual order, associations first, then by name.
