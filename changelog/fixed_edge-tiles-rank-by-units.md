- The "Used by" and "Depends on" tiles rank their groups by distinct units, the count each row shows.
  Before, a unit that reached the page by several edges counted once per edge.
  A row of four units could sit above a row of eight, and a larger group could fall into "other".
  The "other" row of "Depends on" no longer repeats a target that a named row links.
  It is omitted when that leaves it empty.
