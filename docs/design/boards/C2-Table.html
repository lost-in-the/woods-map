Checkbox top, chevron bottom in the first cell, whatever the row height. Hover and checked colour only the toggle cell (the checkbox column), so the colour is a row marker and never sits under text; the steps and the entry point stay plain. Borders are identical open or closed: the row keeps its bottom rule and the body sits under it with its own band. Numbering stays flat to match the diagram; a hierarchical 1 › 1a 1b scheme would encode depth twice (the indent already does) and could not be matched by Mermaid's autonumber, so the async boundary is a labelled rule instead. Keyboard: j / k move rows, enter toggles, space checks, e expands all, E collapses all. Toggle: a bare + or −, Fira Code, slate, purple+ on hover, no box; the checkbox above it in a 34px column padded 8px. Touch: the row is the hit area, the toggle grows to 44px. State is in the URL (open rows, checked set) so a link shares exactly this view.
